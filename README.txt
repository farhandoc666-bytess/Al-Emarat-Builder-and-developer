HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. Double-click index.html (or START_WEBSITE.bat on Windows).
3. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.

HOW TO UPLOAD TO HOSTING
1. Open hosting File Manager / cPanel.
2. Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and the entire assets folder
   into public_html (or your website's configured web root).
3. Connect your domain and HTTPS certificate in your hosting control panel.
4. Check image/video playback and WhatsApp links on the live domain.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- The property cards are SERVICE CATEGORIES, not verified live listings.
  No active listing prices, unit details, claims of ownership, or false
  availability have been fabricated from the uploaded materials.
- This is a static frontend, not a WordPress property-listing database.
  For staff to add, edit, and deactivate property listings through a
  secure admin dashboard, a CMS or backend must be connected.
- Contact and seller forms are not server-side mail/database forms.
  They prepare a message and OPEN WhatsApp. The visitor must press Send.
- Images/videos provided by the user are labelled as reference/media
  showcase, not as verified Al Emarat-completed building projects.
  Confirm you have permission to publish the footage/photographs,
  including any third-party watermarks, before making the site public.
- Any construction quotes, market rates, certifications, legal assurances,
  customer reviews, and verified listings need real company data first.
